Welcome to La' Guerta Adventure Park

Welcome To Our Resort

La' Guerta Adventure Park is a scenic getaway nestled in the heart of nature, offering an escape into lush landscapes and exciting outdoor activities. Located in Zone 3, Sitio Pole Diutay, La Paz, Zamboanga City, Zamboanga del Sur, our resort provides thrilling adventures like ATV rides, basketball, tennis, and billiards, as well as serene spots for relaxation.

Perfect for family gatherings, celebrations, or a refreshing retreat, La' Guerta Adventure Park combines the beauty of the outdoors with memorable activities for all ages.
